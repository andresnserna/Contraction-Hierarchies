Instance manifest - parse self-check
======================================================

If your loader disagrees with any number below, you have a parsing
bug. Fix that before you trust a single measurement: a loader that
misses shelters will produce an E1 ratio that looks plausible and is
wrong, which is the most expensive way to spend an evening on this
project.

These are counts, not answers. Which of them governs the cost of
which approach is the 3.1 question, and it is yours to work out.

shelter with a nonzero node cost, you are reading the wrong column.
